01 IDENTITY

Not just a service.
A place to belong.

CSI Sydney Youth is part of CSI Church Sydney, a congregation shaped by faith, fellowship and the wider story of the Church of South India. The youth ministry creates space for young people to worship, build friendships, talk honestly about faith and step into leadership.
