01 / WHO WE ARE

PART OF CSI CHURCH SYDNEY
175 WALTERS ROAD / BLACKTOWN

Not just a service.
A place to belong.

CSI Sydney Youth is part of CSI Church Sydney, a congregation shaped by faith, fellowship and the wider story of the Church of South India. The youth ministry creates space for young people to worship, build friendships, talk honestly about faith and step into leadership.

03 / FAITH + COMMUNITY

Faith that leaves
room for real life.

The youth fellowship describes itself as a warm, welcoming and safe environment for open discussion and dialogue. Young people are encouraged to take leadership in the life of the church, including readings, hymns, music and other parts of the monthly English service.
